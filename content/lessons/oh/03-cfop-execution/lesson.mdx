CFOP recognition stays the same in OH. The useful change is choosing work that keeps the cube in a comfortable grip. During F2L, favour a visible pair whose insertion uses your confident faces and protects completed slots. Accept a short rotation when it avoids a long, unstable rescue sequence.

<Cube setup="R U R'" alg="R U' R'" controls="true" label="Standard F2L reference insertion with one-handed replay" />

The displayed notation is a **standard/reference** sequence. Its OH-friendly alternative is not assumed from the letters alone: a personal alternative must be verified for the same case before it is saved. The workbench ranks verified alternatives by move count, rotations, slices, regrips and your own comfort rating, then lets you compare two in alternating timed trials.

For last layer, run recognition and execution separately. First identify the picture without turning. Then replay its verified sequence slowly, marking each regrip you need. Only combine the two when each part is dependable.
