Use short blocks. Spend one block identifying cases without turning, one executing a known sequence slowly, and one doing mixed solves with a generous accuracy goal. Record drops, lockups and regrips separately from a wrong case recognition; they need different fixes.

<Cube alg="R U R' U' R' F R2 U' R' U' R U R' F'" controls="true" label="Longer last-layer sequence for one-handed pacing practice" />

In the algorithm workbench, open a verified case and add finger-trick notes, a regrip estimate and a comfort rating. Save only an algorithm the engine accepts for that case. Then use alternating trials to compare two choices; keep enough successful attempts before deciding that one is truly faster.

For a standard/reference sequence, look for rotations, slice turns and where the grip resets. An **OH-friendly candidate** is a personal, verified alternative that you have shown is repeatable on your own hand and cube. It is deliberately not presented as a universal replacement.
