Use short blocks. Spend one block **naming cases without turning**, one **executing a known algorithm slowly**, and one **doing mixed solves** with a generous accuracy goal. Record drops, lockups and regrips separately from a wrong recognition: they need different fixes.

## Compare, then decide

If two algorithms solve the same case, do not pick by turn count. Do a few timed, clean repetitions of each on your own cube, alternating, and keep the one you can repeat without a regrip that catches. Your hand and your cube decide, not the notation.

- Time **recognition** and **execution** separately. They improve at different speeds.
- Count **drops and lockups** as a separate score from time.
- Keep a **weak cases** list. The recognition practice has a filter for exactly that.

## Practise
