This is the whole thing, once, slowly. No timer, no blindfold yet: the site walks you through a real scramble and shows every step. Do it on a real cube alongside if you have one.

## 1. Memo

Trace the edges and the corners, split them into pairs, and note whether the count is odd. The walkthrough below shows the memo for its scramble first; cover it and trace the scramble yourself before you look.

## 2. Execute

Press Start. Each step shows its setup, swap and undo, animated from exactly where the previous step left the cube, with the pieces it moves lit:

- first every **edge** target, in memo order;
- then the **parity** step, because this scramble has an odd count;
- then every **corner** target.

## 3. Then do it blind

Once the walkthrough makes sense, try the same scramble on a real cube: memorise it, blindfold on, and solve. If it doesn't come out, don't start again from scratch. Check, in this order:

1. **The memo.** Trace it again with your eyes; does it match what you remembered?
2. **The setups.** Did every undo exactly reverse its setup?
3. **Parity.** Was the count odd, and did you do the parity step between edges and corners?

Most failed early solves are one wrong letter or one missed undo. The practice section has a guided trace trainer for the first, and an Old Pochmann trainer for setups.
