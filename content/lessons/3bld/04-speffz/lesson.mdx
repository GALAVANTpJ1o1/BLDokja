A blindfolded memo is a list of places. Saying "the front sticker of the top-front-right corner" every time would be hopeless, so every sticker gets a single letter. The scheme this site uses by default is called **Speffz**.

## Two alphabets, one rule

Corners and edges each get their own A to X: 24 letters for 24 stickers. The same letter means a corner sticker when you're tracing corners and an edge sticker when you're tracing edges. You'll never mix them up, because you trace one kind at a time.

Both alphabets follow the same rule:

1. The faces are lettered in the order **U, L, F, R, B, D**. Each face gets four letters: U gets A to D, L gets E to H, F gets I to L, R gets M to P, B gets Q to T, and D gets U to X.
2. On each face, the letters go **clockwise, starting at the top-left**, as you look at that face with the cube held white on top and green in front.
3. On the top and bottom faces, "top-left" means the corner furthest from you on the left (for U) and nearest you on the left (for D), the way they sit in a net.

So on the top face the corners are <Letter sticker="UBL" /> back-left, <Letter sticker="UBR" /> back-right, <Letter sticker="UFR" /> front-right and <Letter sticker="UFL" /> front-left.

## Edges follow the corners

Each edge sticker takes the letter of the corner sticker just before it, going clockwise. On the top face that makes the back edge <Letter sticker="UB" />, the right edge <Letter sticker="UR" />, the front edge <Letter sticker="UF" /> and the left edge <Letter sticker="UL" />.

You don't need to memorise that separately. Find the corner letters on a face, then each edge shares the letter of the corner just counterclockwise of it.

## Try it

Choose corners or edges, then press stickers. The letters are printed on the net; the colours are the faces they belong to. Start with the top face, then walk around L, F, R, B and finish on D.
