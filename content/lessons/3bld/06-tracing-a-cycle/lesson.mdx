Tracing is how a scramble becomes a list of letters. You do it with your eyes, before the blindfold, and you never make a move while you do it.

## The loop

Tracing copies what shooting will do, in your head:

1. Look at the **buffer**. Read the sticker in it and ask: where does this sticker belong? That place's letter is your first target.
2. Look at **that** place on the cube. Whatever sticker is sitting there belongs somewhere too. Its home's letter is your next target.
3. Keep going. Each target tells you where to look for the next one.

The chain stops when the sticker you read belongs to the buffer's own piece. Everything you've read so far forms a **cycle**, and every piece in it is accounted for.

The trick is step 2: you read what's **physically in** the place you just named, on the scrambled cube. You're not moving anything; you're following arrows.
