To memorise a cube you've got to be able to say exactly what's where. Step one: know what the cube is actually made of.

## Three kinds of piece

A cube has 26 pieces you can see:

- **8 corners**, each with 3 stickers.
- **12 edges**, each with 2 stickers.
- **6 centres**, each with 1 sticker.

That's 24 corner stickers, 24 edge stickers and 6 centre stickers: 54 total. Tiny squares, big lore.

Here's one corner lit up. It's where the top, front and right faces meet, and all three of its stickers are glowing.

<Cube highlight="UFR FUR RUF" label="The top-front-right corner, with its three stickers lit" />

## Hold it the same way, every time

Face turns never move the centres, so on a 3x3 the centres tell you which face is which. Blind solvers hold the cube the same way every time: **white on top, green in front**. That puts red on the right, orange on the left, blue at the back and yellow underneath. Lock it in.

Held like that, every spot has a fixed name. Top is U (up), then D (down), F (front), B (back), R (right) and L (left).

A piece gets named by the faces it touches. The corner above is UFR: up, front, right. The edge between top and front is UF.

## Why stickers, not pieces

Say UFR is sitting in its own spot but twisted. It can be in there three ways: white facing up, front, or right. If you only said "UFR is here", you'd lose which way it's turned, and that detail really matters later.

A sticker covers both at once. So each **sticker** gets named by its own face first, then the rest of its piece:

- UFR is the corner's top sticker.
- FUR is its front sticker.
- RUF is its right sticker.

Here's the same corner with only its front sticker, FUR, lit.

<Cube highlight="FUR" label="Only the front sticker of the top-front-right corner, FUR, lit" />

Edges work the same with two stickers: UF is the top sticker of the top-front edge, and FU is its front sticker.

<Cube highlight="UF FU" label="The top-front edge, with both of its stickers lit" />

From here on, "the sticker in UFR" means a spot on the cube, not a colour. Whatever sticker is chilling in that spot right now is what you read.
