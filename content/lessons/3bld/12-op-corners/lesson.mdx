Time to shoot corners for real. Everything here is one swap sequence and a handful of setups.

## The swap

The corner swap exchanges the buffer, <Buffer method="op" pieces="corners" />, with the swap spot, the right sticker of the bottom-front-right corner: RDF, <Letter sticker="RDF" /> in Speffz. As a side effect it also swaps two top-layer edges, UB and UL.

<SwapAlg pieces="corners" />

Learn it until your hands do it without you. It's 15 moves, and you'll do it for every corner target.

A target sitting on the swap spot needs no setup at all:

## Setups

Every other target needs a setup: moves that bring **that target sticker** to the swap spot, RDF. The rule for choosing them:

- Use only **D, R and F** turns.
- Keep it short: every corner target has a setup of at most two moves.
- Undo it straight after the swap, in reverse.

Two examples, one on the top layer and one underneath:

<OpShot pieces="corners" target="UFR" />

<OpShot pieces="corners" target="DFR" />

Notice that the setup has to bring the exact sticker, not just the piece, to RDF. The same corner needs different setups for its three stickers.

## Why U, L and B are off limits

The swap only works if the buffer and its side-effect edges, UB and UL, are where it expects them. A U turn moves the buffer and both edges; L moves the buffer and UL; B moves the buffer and UB. A setup using them can bring the target to the swap spot and still wreck the solve.

<Note>You don't need to memorise a table of 21 setups. For any target, ask: which D, R or F turns bring this sticker to RDF? Try it, check it on a real cube, and the setups become obvious within a week.</Note>
