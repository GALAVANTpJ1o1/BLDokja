You don't need a new scheme for a 4x4. Speffz already has 24 letters per piece type, and a 4x4 has 24 wings and 24 x-centres. Each gets one letter, following patterns you already know.

## Wings follow the edges

On each face, a 3x3's edge letters go clockwise from the back. A 4x4 face has two wing stickers on each of its four edges; the letters go clockwise too, and each face's letter lands on the first wing sticker reached going clockwise round that edge. On the U face that's **A** at the back, **B** on the right, **C** at the front and **D** on the left, just like the edges UB, UR, UF and UL.

Sticker names say the same thing. A wing sticker is named by its face, then the edge it's on, then a lower-case letter for the side of the edge it sits towards. So **UBl** is the U-face sticker of the wing on the UB edge, towards the L side. It's <FourLetter sticker="UBl" />.

## X-centres follow the corners

Each face's four x-centres take the letters of that face's corners, clockwise from the back left. On the U face: **A** back left, **B** back right, **C** front right, **D** front left. An x-centre's name is its face in capitals and the two sides it's nearest to in lower case, so **Ubl** is the U x-centre nearest back and left: <FourLetter sticker="Ubl" />.

Press stickers to check. Switch between wings, x-centres and corners with the buttons.

## The buffers

The 4BLD lessons and trainers use these buffers:

- **x-centres:** Ubr, the U-face x-centre back right, <FourLetter sticker="Ubr" />.
- **wings:** the wing between D and F on the right side. Its lettered sticker is FDr, <FourLetter sticker="FDr" />.
- **corners:** UBL, the same Old Pochmann buffer as on a 3x3, <FourLetter sticker="UBL" />.

Why FDr and not DFr? A wing can't be flipped, so only one of its two stickers ever needs a letter, and Speffz letters the one on the F face here.
