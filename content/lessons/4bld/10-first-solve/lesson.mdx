This is a whole 4BLD solve, slowly, with nothing hidden. The scramble is short, so every phase has only a few targets, and it happens to have an odd count in all three, so you'll see every parity step once.

## 1. Memo

Hold the cube as it was scrambled. Trace the x-centres, then the wings, then the corners, and note for each whether the count is odd. The walkthrough shows the memo first; cover it and trace the scramble yourself before you look.

## 2. Execute

Press Start. Every step is animated from exactly where the last one left the cube:

- each **x-centre** target, then **U2** because the count is odd;
- each **wing** target, then the **r2 parity alg**;
- each **corner** target, then the **corner parity alg**.

<FourSolve scramble="2U D 2U 2B' 2U2" />

## 3. Then do it blind

Once the walkthrough makes sense, do the same scramble on a real 4x4: memorise it, blindfold on, solve. Then practise tracing full scrambles in the 4BLD trainer before you try one blind, and expect that first full solve to take a while.

If a solve doesn't come out, look at the cube before you start again. Its state says a lot:

1. **Centres wrong.** Something went wrong in the first phase, and everything after it was done on a cube that didn't match the memo.
2. **The U layer's corners and wings a half turn out.** A missed or extra centre parity U2.
3. **Two wings swapped, with x-centres out of place.** A missed wing parity step.
4. **The UB and UL wing pairs swapped.** A missed corner parity step.
5. **A few pieces of one type cycled.** A memo mistake, or an undo that didn't exactly reverse its setup.
