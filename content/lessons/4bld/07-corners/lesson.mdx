Good news: you already know how to solve these. A 4x4's corners are exactly a 3x3's corners, and Old Pochmann turns only outer faces, so every setup, the swap and every undo work unchanged.

## What the swap does to wings

On a 3x3, the corner swap also swaps the UB and UL edges. A 4x4 has no single UB edge: it has two wings there, and two on UL. So on a 4x4 the corner swap swaps **the pair of wings on UB with the pair on UL**. Watch the lit wings on the U face in the shot above.

Like on a 3x3, that cancels when the corner targets come in pairs. After an odd number, the corners are solved and those two pairs of wings are left swapped. Lesson 8 fixes that.

The swap also moves some x-centres, but only between slots of the same face. Your centres are solved by the time you do corners, so nothing you can see changes.

## Trace them

Corners are traced exactly as on a 3x3, from the cube held the way it was scrambled. Twisted corners are traced as targets, as Old Pochmann always does.
