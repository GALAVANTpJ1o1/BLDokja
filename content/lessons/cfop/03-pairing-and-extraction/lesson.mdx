A disconnected pair is not an insertion problem yet. First put both pieces where you can see them, then make a block on U. Keep the cross on D and use the side centres to decide the destination.

<MoveExplorer moves="R U R' U' F U F'" label="F2L preparation moves: turn one move at a time" />

## Extract before forcing

If a corner is trapped in its own slot with the matching edge above it, open that slot just enough to lift the corner to U. Turn U to keep the edge away, then close the slot. The point is not a fixed sequence: it is leaving the cross and every finished slot as you found them.

<Cube setup="R U R'" alg="U' R U' R'" controls="true" label="A short F2L preparation sequence, shown with replay controls" />

## Pair on U

Name the corner's D colour and its two side colours. Find the edge with those two side colours. Turn U until the stickers that must touch form matching stripes. If the pair would point straight into its slot, move it away first; this gives your opening move somewhere safe to work.

Prefer a short, explainable preparation over rushing into an insertion. On a physical cube, stop after the pairing move and check both pieces; that habit prevents most accidental cross damage.
