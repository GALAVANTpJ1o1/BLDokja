Full PLL reads all piece destinations together. Start by finding a solved block or a pair of headlights, then follow the remaining edge cycle. Before executing, predict which U adjustment will make the result solved.

<LastLayerExplorer kind="pll" />

There are 21 computed full PLL recognition classes here. The compact list is intentionally a searchable table, not 21 repeated cards: select a case to see notation, its recognition state and an animated replay. Personal alternatives belong in the algorithm workbench, where they can be saved, rated and compared.

An ordinary 3×3 has no separate PLL parity algorithm. If the last layer appears to have one swapped pair, stop and inspect the earlier solve or the cube assembly; do not import a 4×4 parity fix.
