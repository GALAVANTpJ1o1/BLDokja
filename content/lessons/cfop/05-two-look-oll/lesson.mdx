For two-look OLL, first read the four U-layer edge stickers. Do not turn just because one top sticker looks unfamiliar; inspect the whole edge pattern. Once the edges are oriented, read the corners as a separate picture.

<LastLayerExplorer kind="eo" compact="true" />

Switch the same viewer to **orient corners** after you can identify the edge picture. Use the case list as recognition practice: say the case identifier first, then play the notation. That keeps recognition timing separate from execution timing.

<LastLayerExplorer kind="co" compact="true" />

The sequences in this project are verified against their stage, including preserved F2L. They are deliberately labelled reference notation; save a verified personal replacement in the algorithm workbench only after checking its exact effect.
