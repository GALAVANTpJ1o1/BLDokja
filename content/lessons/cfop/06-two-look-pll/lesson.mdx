PLL moves pieces without changing the solved orientation. Check the top stickers first: if they are not oriented, return to OLL instead of trying to diagnose permutation colours.

In two-look PLL, follow the corners to their home slots, then follow the edges. A final U adjustment is a normal part of recognising the finished state, not evidence that the algorithm failed.
