After OLL the top is all yellow, so **the top face cannot help you any more**. Everything you need is on the **sides of the top layer**: the three stickers each side face shows along its top row. Two-look PLL reads them in two looks: **corners first, then edges**.

## What to look for

- **Headlights:** the two corners on one side show the **same colour** on that side. Two matching stickers with a different colour between them, like a car's lights.
- **A bar:** all **three** stickers on one side match. A solved bar.

Both are drawn on the cards below, with an outline around the side that carries them (solid for a bar, dashed for headlights), so you can find them without relying on colour. Each sticker also carries the first letter of its colour.

## Look one: the corners

The corners are permuted when every corner shows its own colours on both sides. There are two starting pictures.

- **Headlights:** two corners already match on one side; the other two do not.
- **Diagonal:** no side has headlights.

<CaseCards stage="cp" />

These two algorithms also move the edges, and that is fine. Once the corners are in place, only the edges are left.

## Look two: the edges

With the corners solved, every side shows headlights. Only the edges are out of place, and there are four pictures: **H**, **Z** and two three-edge cycles, **Ua** and **Ub**.

<CaseCards stage="ep" />

## The last turn

The layer can end up a quarter turn out. After the corners, and again at the very end, turn **U** until the corner colours line up with the centres. It is easy to forget, and it is a whole extra step if you do: the cards say when one is needed.

## Recognise before you turn

In the practice you see a case, name it, and then the algorithm plays on the same cube, so what the corner algorithm leaves behind is the edge picture you see next. Timed recognition and execution are separate skills, so they are kept separate.
