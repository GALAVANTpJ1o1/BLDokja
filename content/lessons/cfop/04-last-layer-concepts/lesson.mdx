The last layer has two jobs. **Orientation** makes every U-layer sticker face the right way; **permutation** puts those oriented pieces in their home positions. A piece can be oriented but still be in the wrong place, so do not mix the questions.

<Cube setup="R U R' U R U2 R'" alg="R U2 R' U' R U' R'" controls="true" label="Last-layer orientation example with full replay controls" />

Two-look last layer handles the jobs in smaller groups: edges then corners for OLL, corners then edges for PLL. Full OLL and PLL recognise the whole picture at once. Both routes finish the same cube; choose the route that matches what you have practised.
