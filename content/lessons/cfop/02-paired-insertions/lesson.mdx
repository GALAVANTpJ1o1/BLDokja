F2L means **first two layers**. With the white cross on the bottom, four **slots** are left, one beside each pair of neighbouring centres. Each slot takes one corner and one edge. Those two pieces are a **pair**, and the whole aim of F2L is to build each pair and put it into its slot without breaking the cross or a slot you have already finished.

On this site CFOP cubes are drawn with **yellow on top and white on the bottom**. The cross is on the bottom, the last layer is the yellow one. (Blindfolded lessons keep white on top, because lettering depends on it.)

## Read the corner to find its slot

A corner has three colours: white, and two side colours. The two side colours are the centres it must sit between. The white-green-orange corner belongs in the slot between the green centre and the orange centre, which is the **front-right slot** on these cubes. Its edge is the one with just those two colours, green and orange. Neither piece cares where it is now; its colours say where it goes.

<Cube setup="R U R'" alg="R U' R'" controls="true" highlight="UFL LUF FUL UF FU" dim="soft" label="The front-right pair, joined in the top layer. The two lit pieces are the pair; press play to insert it." />

Look at what is lit. The corner and the edge sit **next to each other with their colours matching**: the edge's green stripe touches the corner's green sticker, and its other stripe touches the other side colour. That is a **joined pair**: two pieces acting as one block.

## The insertion: open, bring over, close

Step through the three turns.

1. **R opens the slot.** The slot is empty now and the cross is disturbed. Do not judge the cube here.
2. **U' carries the joined pair over the empty slot.** Both pieces move together, so they stay joined.
3. **R' closes the slot.** The pair drops in, the cross comes back, and the other three slots are exactly as you left them.

The pair only lands in the right place because the two pieces travelled as one block. Everything else in F2L is about getting to this picture.

## Three ways the white sticker can face

When the corner is in the top layer, its white sticker faces one of three directions. This is the most useful thing to read at a glance, because it tells you what the pair needs. Here are all three with the corner in the same place, front-right on top.

**White facing the right side.** The edge is behind the corner. Three turns pair it and insert it.

<Cube setup="R U' R'" alg="R U R'" controls="true" highlight="UFR FUR RUF UB BU" dim="soft" label="White facing the right side. R lifts the corner beside its edge, U carries the joined pair over the slot, R prime closes it." />

R lifts the corner up next to its edge, so they join. U carries the joined pair over the slot. R' closes it.

**White facing the front.** Here the pair is already joined, sitting over the slot. If you opened the slot straight away, the pair would go with it, so first move it out of the way.

<Cube setup="R U R' U'" alg="U R U' R'" controls="true" highlight="UFR FUR RUF UR RU" dim="soft" label="White facing the front, pair already joined over the slot. U moves it aside, R opens the slot, U prime brings the pair back, R prime closes." />

U moves the pair aside. R opens the slot. U' brings the pair back over it. R' closes it: **move out of the way, open, bring back, close**.

**White facing up.** The corner cannot go straight in this way. It has to be turned into one of the two pictures you already know.

<Cube setup="R U' R' U R U2 R'" alg="R U2 R' U' R U R'" controls="true" highlight="UFR FUR RUF UR RU" dim="soft" label="White facing up. The first four turns change the picture into the white-facing-the-side case, then the last three insert it." />

Watch the first four turns, **R U2 R' U'**. They swing the edge round to the back and turn the corner so that it is in the same place but its white sticker now faces the side. That is exactly the first picture: white facing the right, edge behind the corner. The last three turns, **R U R'**, are the insertion you have already seen. You never had to learn a new sequence: you **changed the situation into one you know**. The next lesson makes that the whole method.

## Before you turn

For any pair, say out loud: the corner's three colours, the edge's two colours, the slot they belong to, and which way the white sticker faces. Only then choose a turn. If you cannot say those four things, look again; guessing an insertion is how crosses get broken.
