Advanced intuitive F2L is not a different set of cube rules. It is a tighter decision loop: scan all four slots, choose a visible pair, keep solved slots out of the working face, then pair before inserting.

<Cube setup="U R U' R'" alg="R U R'" controls="true" label="F2L pairing and insertion demonstration with move controls" />

Before each turn, say what it is protecting and what it is moving. If a move sends a completed pair into the working layer, choose a different slot or rotate your plan rather than repairing after every pair.

<MoveExplorer moves="U U' R R' F F' L L'" label="Advanced F2L planning moves" />

Speed appears when recognition and execution are clean independently: time your decision while the cube is visible, then time the prepared sequence without searching mid-execution.
