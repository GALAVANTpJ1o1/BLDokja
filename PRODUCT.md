# BLDokja

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Beginners learning blindfolded cubing and experienced solvers practising accuracy, recognition, memo and execution. The sighted CFOP path assumes an existing beginner-method solve; it does not teach that method.

## Product Purpose

Teach and practise 3BLD, the differences for 4BLD, and progressively introduce 5BLD piece families. Interactive cubes and verified steps connect the lesson to what the learner sees on a physical cube.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Local-only, no accounts, backend or telemetry. User schemes, buffers, images, words, algorithms and progress remain on the device behind a storage port with backups. Algorithms are computationally verified before shipping. CFOP order confirmed September 17: notation, beginner F2L, 2-look OLL/PLL, advanced intuitive F2L, full OLL/PLL.

## Brand Commitments

The cube remains the hero. The user requested richer selectable palettes and optional galaxy, rain, snow, forest and an additional ambient environment. These extend the established identity rather than replacing the teaching interface. The rare Indian flag is allowed only as a verified normal-cube move sequence, never as painted stickers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operation, readable mobile layouts, colourblind cube palettes, text cube representation, optional voice with fallback, offline support, and reduced-motion behaviour.

## Evidence on Hand

BRIEF.md, docs/ENGINE.md, docs/DESIGN.md, verified engine/content datasets, unit tests and Playwright checks. Physical-cube checks and real iOS checks are not inferred from browser emulation.
